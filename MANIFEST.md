## 💾 Database Schema (4 Tables)

```sql
✅ users
   - id, name, email, password, phone, role
   - UNIQUE: email
   - Relations: 1 User → Many Products, Warranties

✅ products
   - id, product_name, brand, model, serial_number
   - purchase_date, price, user_id
   - UNIQUE: serial_number
   - Relations: Many Products → 1 User, 1 Warranty

✅ warranty
   - id, product_id, user_id
   - start_date, expiry_date, status
   - UNIQUE: product_id
   - Relations: 1 Warranty → 1 Product, Many Claims

✅ claims
   - id, warranty_id, claim_date
   - issue_description, status, admin_remarks
   - Relations: Many Claims → 1 Warranty
```
